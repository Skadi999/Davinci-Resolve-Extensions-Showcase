Is there a free trial?

Yes. Download the installer and use every feature — transcribing, styles, editing and the live preview — for as long as you like. A license unlocks adding the captions to your timeline and exporting subtitles.

Does it work with the free version of DaVinci Resolve?

No. DaVinci Resolve only runs extensions like Onyx Captions in DaVinci Resolve Studio, version 21.1 or newer.

Which operating systems are supported?

Onyx Captions currently only supports Windows 10/11. MacOS support is planned.

What exactly do I receive after buying?

A license key and a download link for the installer. Both arrive by email immediately after checkout and are also shown on the order confirmation page. If you've already installed the free trial, paste the key into Onyx Captions — no reinstall needed.

How do I install, update or uninstall it?

In DaVinci Resolve Studio, open Workspace › Console and drag Install Onyx Captions.lua into the Console window. Click Install, then restart Resolve and open Workspace › Workflow Integrations › Onyx Captions. To update, do the same with the newer file; to uninstall, drag it in again and click Uninstall. The installer runs inside Resolve, so there's nothing to run outside it and no admin rights are needed.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to activate your key and to confirm it about once a day. Without internet it keeps working for 14 days after the last check. Your footage and transcripts never leave your computer.
